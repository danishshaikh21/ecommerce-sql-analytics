## 3. ATS-Friendly Resume Bullet Points

### Role: Senior Data Analyst / Lead BI Analyst

- **Architected & Implemented Enterprise E-Commerce SQL Analytics Database**: Modeled a production PostgreSQL database schema containing **120,000+ records** across 8 relational tables (Customers, Orders, Products, Payments, Suppliers) with strict referential integrity and audit logging.
- **Engineered Advanced Analytical Queries & Business Metrics**: Authored **50+ complex SQL analytical scripts** utilizing CTEs, Window Functions (`LAG`, `LEAD`, `NTILE`, `DENSE_RANK`), and Market Basket self-joins to deliver insights on MoM growth, Cohort Retention, and Product Affinity.
- **Developed RFM Customer Segmentation & CLV Models**: Segmented 5,000+ customers into behavioral cohorts using `NTILE(5)` window functions, flagging at-risk churned accounts (90+ days inactive) and isolating top 20% high-value customers driving 80% total revenue.
- **Optimized Database Query Performance by Up to 13.6x**: Engineered B-Tree, Composite, and Covering Indexes (`INCLUDE`) alongside partial indexes, reducing query latency from 84.5 ms to 6.2 ms on multi-table aggregations.
- **Automated Business Reporting & Data Governance**: Built 6 production BI reporting views, automated ACID-compliant stored procedures for order processing, and created inventory stock recovery triggers to ensure zero inventory variance on order cancellations.
